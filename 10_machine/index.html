This assignment was definitely one of the hardest assignments. It required a lot of different skill sets that we learned in this class along with new ones to come together so that the drawing robot could be built. <br> To start off this assignment my team and I first brainstormed what we wanted to build. Later on this idea was mostly discared and we decided we simply wanted to make a drawing robot that could draw different shapes, designs, and patterns based off of a grid/code on the computer.
