<div class="textcontainer"> <p class="margin"> </p> <h3>Week 8: CNC Milling</h3> <h4>Assignment: Make Something With CNC</h4> 1. CNC MILLING 2. Modlding/Casting/ Vaccum molding <br> 1. To start off this first part of the assignment I used the Shopbot to do some CNC milling. I decided that I wanted to make a heart shaped jewlery box that could actually store some of my jewlery like necklaces and rings. Unfortunately there was only so much wood I could use and the thickness of the wood in the shop was a bit smaller than I imagined so my orginal depth of the jewlery box was going to be 50mm and I had to bring it down to about 12 mm. But regardless the jewlery box turned out how I wanted it to. First I had to create a cad of my jewlery box so I did just that. <br> Dimensions from user parameters: <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="parameters-1 (1).jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="parameters-2 (1).jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> The user parameters were very helpful for this particular project as it created a easy quick system to chnage dimensions and for the design in Fusion to "scale itself" without me having to manually go back into sketches and change dimensions one by one. Again, due to the maximum thickness of the wood available in the shop I did have to change my user parameters. I looked up clipart images of hearts and then replicated it from there to create both a bottom and a lid. The bottom is designed to be smaller than the lid so that the lid can actually funtion as such. So, when you close the box the lid fits over the bottom perfectly. <br> <br> Here are some images of the CAD <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="heart-1.jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="heart-2.jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> DOWNLOAD CAD FILES: <br> <a download href='./Heart box.f3d'> 3D MODEL file </a> <br> <br> Aftering creating a CAD of the heart jelwery box I went over to the Shopbot to cut out both the bottom piece and the lid piece with the help of Bobby. First wood was picked out, then the size of the wood was measured so the computer program had an idea of the wood size for calibration. Then the wood was nailed down firmly in place so it wouldn't move while the Shopbot was completing the job. Once all of this was completed it was time to do an "aircut" to make sure the program correctly understood dimensions and that the piece being cut was cut properly within the space. The aircut was successful so it was time to cut the pieces! <br> Picture of the Shopbot: <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="shop-bot.jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> Picture of the cuts: <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="cut-1.jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> After the pieces were both cut(bottom and lid) I was able to take them out and I went over to the Sanding machien to sand the edges of each peice so that they would look clean and smooth. Espically since prior to sanding it was overwhemingly easy to get splinters. <br> Picture of the sander I used: <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="sander.jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> Once completing the sanding of the pieces they were smooth ands much more appealing/nice to look at. Since I had an engraving of my intials I created in my CAD I went over to the Lasercutter and scored the intials onto the lid of the jewlery box and then the wooden jewlery box was done! Some next steps would be to paint the jewlery box or make it glossy for an appealing visual effect. <br> Here are photos of the wooden jewelry box completed: <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="final_j_box.jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> <img style="border: 3px solid #eb85e7; box-shadow: #d53aa8 5px 5px 7px 4px;" src="final_j_box_1.jpeg" alt="generic placeholder image"> <br> <br> 2.
